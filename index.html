Shia Rishta — nikah-first Shia matrimony

Shia Rishta is a matchmaking platform built for Shia Muslims seeking nikah, and for the families and walis who support them. Every profile is created with marriage intent. Photos stay private by default, conversations require mutual interest, and family involvement is part of the journey rather than an afterthought.

Free to join. There is no payment system today and no paid tier is required to browse, express interest, message a match, or use the guardian tools.

Privacy by design

Your contact details are never published. A member chooses exactly who sees their photos: everyone, only after mutual interest, or only after matching. Blocking is instant, and revoking a wali or guardian link takes effect immediately.

Family and wali workflows

In Shia fiqh a virgin woman’s nikah requires her wali’s consent, so guardian approvals, family-to-family introductions and chaperoned communication are built in from the start. A member can delegate profile management to a parent, guardian or trusted matchmaker.

How it works

1. Create a profile with your intent

Set your marriage goals, religious practice, family background and timeline, plus the sect and marja filters that generic matrimonial sites do not carry.

2. Browse and express interest privately

Search by sect, city, age and practice. Interest is one-sided until it is mutual, so nobody is notified of an unreciprocated approach.

3. Talk once you both agree

A conversation opens only after mutual interest. Contact exchange and photo unlocks require agreement from both sides.

Who can join

Serious Shia Muslims seeking nikah, along with parents, guardians and trusted matchmakers acting on their behalf. Identity and intention checks are reviewed before sensitive access opens.

Common questions

Is Shia Rishta really free?

Yes. Creating a profile, browsing, expressing interest and using guardian tools are all free, and core access is intended to stay free.

Who can see my photos?

Only the people you choose: everyone, people you have mutual interest with, or only your matches. The default is the most private of those.

Can my family be involved?

Yes. Wali and guardian workflows are first-class: approvals, family introductions and chaperoned communication.

Contact

Questions, privacy requests or takedown requests go through our contact form, which reaches the operations team directly. Signed-in members can also raise a support request from their dashboard.
